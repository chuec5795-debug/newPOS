Common questions:

New to Store POS System? Start here

1

How do I process sales & scan barcodes?

Use the Sales tab. Scan item barcodes with your hardware scanner or manually search products by name to add them to the cart.

2

How does the Digital QR Receipt work?

After completing a checkout, a digital QR code is generated on screen for the customer to scan with their phone camera to view their bill.

3

How to manage & update Inventory stock?

Managers can navigate to the Inventory section to add new items, update prices, or restock quantities stored in LocalStorage.

4

Where can I see Low Stock Alerts?

When an item quantity falls below the minimum limit, a warning badge appears in the Dashboard and Low Stock section automatically.

5

Login and User Role Access details

Admin accounts can create Cashier and Manager roles. Ensure correct credentials and role selection when logging into the system.

6

How do I add or manage user accounts?

Admins and Managers can navigate to Account Management (accounts.html) from the sidebar to create new accounts by providing an email address, password, and selecting a role (Sales, Manager, or Admin). Existing user permissions and accounts can also be reviewed or removed from this page.

7

How do customers access their digital receipts?

Upon clicking "Finish & Get Digital Receipt", the system converts the transaction data into a Base64-encoded string parameter and generates a QR code. Customers can scan the QR code using their phone camera to open and view their digital receipt page (receipt.html).

8

How is stock quantity updated during a transaction?

When a transaction is completed, the system automatically subtracts the purchased item quantities from the total available inventory saved in local storage (myInventory)

9

What payment methods are supported at checkout?

1. Cash
2. KBZPay/Mobile Wallet
3. Wavepay
4. Credit/Debit Card

10

How does the Cashier POS Terminal work for scanning items?

Cashiers can scan item barcodes using a physical scanner. The system detects the barcode input, prompts for the desired quantity, checks if sufficient inventory is available, and adds the product to the active cart.
